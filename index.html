Sector expertise

Built for environments where the stakes are real

From collapsed structures to contaminated sites, our solutions are shaped by the operational realities of five critical sectors.

Emergency Services

Robotic reconnaissance ahead of personnel entry into collapsed structures, fires and hazmat incidents — for fire & rescue, police and SAR teams.

Infrastructure

Automated inspection for rail, utilities and water networks — cutting cost and downtime while improving data quality and HSE compliance.

Hazmat & Nuclear

Autonomous monitoring and response systems that keep people out of contaminated or radioactive environments while maintaining capability.

Mining

Autonomous surveying and hazard assessment that opens up exploration in underground environments too dangerous for routine personnel access.

Government & Policy Bodies

Innovation partnerships and funded deployment routes that bring proven robotics capability into the public sector cost-effectively.
