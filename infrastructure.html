Where robotics fits

Capability across inspection and maintenance

Inspection drones

Aerial and confined-space inspection that removes personnel from high-risk structures, towers and enclosed assets.

Maintenance robots

Robotic platforms for cleaning, repair and routine intervention in environments that are costly or dangerous to access manually.

Autonomous monitoring

Continuous sensor-based monitoring of structural and mechanical health, feeding real-time data into your operations centre.
