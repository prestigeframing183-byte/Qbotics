Our approach

Four service tiers, one accountable partner

We don't sell a single product. We start with your operational challenge and recommend the tier that matches where you are today — from a first honest assessment through to a fully custom, supported system.

New to robotics, or just exploring?

Start with Tier 1 — Consultation & Assessment. You'll get a clear, honest answer on feasibility before committing further budget.

Go to Tier 2 — Solution Customisation. Faster deployment, lower risk, proven technology adapted to your operation.
