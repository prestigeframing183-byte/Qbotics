Agriculture & Food Production

Harvesting 40% More Crop With 50% Less Labour

Agricultural producer (case study preview)

Challenge

Labour shortages during a critical harvest window, rising labour costs, and post-harvest processing bottlenecks were capping the site's sellable output.

Solution

Custom autonomous harvesting robots with computer-vision ripeness assessment, automated sorting and grading, integrated with the farm's management software, plus operator training.

40%yield increase
50%labour reduction
35%throughput improvement
↑growing operations expanded
“We went into harvest expecting to be short-staffed. Instead we processed more than ever, with a team half the size.”
Cost: £120kTimeline: 8 weeksROI: ~14 months

Infrastructure & Maintenance

Reducing Infrastructure Downtime by 60% Through Autonomous Monitoring

Infrastructure operator (case study preview)

Challenge

Ageing infrastructure needing constant maintenance, reactive repairs creating costly emergencies, high-risk inspection environments and a shortage of skilled technicians.

Solution

Autonomous inspection and monitoring drones, real-time asset-health sensor integration, a predictive maintenance analytics platform tied into the existing maintenance management system, and field technician training.

60%downtime reduction
45%maintenance cost reduction
20–30%asset lifespan extension
